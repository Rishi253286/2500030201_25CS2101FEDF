About PawCare

Saving Lives, One Adoption at a Time

The PawCare Pet Adoption System was founded to eliminate animal homelessness and encourage ethical adoption. We rescue injured, lost, and abandoned pets, provide veterinary healthcare, microchipping, and sterilization, and connect them with loving adopters.
